## <span id="why-project">👥 Why HR Attrition Insights?</span>  

Employee attrition directly impacts **talent retention, workforce stability, and organizational performance.**  
This Tableau dashboard provides an **enterprise-ready view** of attrition patterns by **age, gender, department, education field, and job role.**  
It enables HR leaders to **identify risks, understand employee behavior, and build strategies** for retention, recruitment, and engagement.  

---

## <span id="project-overview">🧩 Project Overview</span>  

The **HR Attrition Insights Dashboard** is a Tableau-powered BI solution that consolidates employee data to deliver **interactive insights** on workforce attrition.  

It highlights **who is leaving, why they are leaving, and where improvements can be made.**  
By combining KPI cards, charts, and demographic breakdowns, it offers a **360° view of workforce health** and supports HR in **data-driven policy-making.**  

---

## <span id="project-context">🏢 Project Context</span>  

Organizations struggle with:  

- High attrition rates leading to increased hiring costs.  
- Poor visibility into attrition by demographics or job roles.  
- Difficulty in linking attrition with satisfaction and department performance.  

This dashboard addresses these issues by:  

- Centralizing employee data.  
- Providing **real-time, visualized attrition trends.**  
- Enabling HR teams to make **faster, evidence-based decisions.**  

---

## <span id="key-considerations">🧠 Key Considerations</span>  

- High **workforce diversity** across departments and roles.  
- Need for **segmentation by gender, education, and age groups.**  
- Focus on **employee satisfaction and retention strategies.**  
- **Scalability** of dashboard for future HR datasets.

## <span id="project-objectives">🎯 Project Objectives</span>  

- **Visualize Attrition Trends**  
  Track overall attrition rate and department-wise breakdown.  

- **Understand Employee Demographics**  
  Analyze attrition by **age group, gender, and education field.**  

- **Measure Job Satisfaction Impact**  
  Correlate attrition with satisfaction levels across job roles.  

- **Support HR Strategies**  
  Enable data-driven decisions to **reduce turnover and improve engagement.**

## <span id="business-problems">🛑 Business Problems Addressed</span>  

### ❌ Problem 1: Lack of visibility  
Attrition was not clearly segmented by age, gender, or department.  

### ❌ Problem 2: Hidden drivers of attrition  
Job satisfaction data was not effectively linked with attrition outcomes.  

### ❌ Problem 3: Reactive HR strategy  
Decisions were based on assumptions, not insights.  

### ✅ Solution Provided 
 
- Interactive Tableau dashboard with **KPI cards + visuals.**  
- Deep-dive into **department, education field, and job role attrition.**  
- **Age group & gender analysis** for targeted retention policies.  

---

## <span id="key-features">💡 Key Features & Visual Insights</span>  

- 📊 **KPI Cards:** Overall employee count, attrition count, attrition rate, active employees, and average age.  
- 🥧 **Department-Wise Attrition:** Pie chart showing HR, R&D, and Sales contribution.  
- 📈 **Employee Age Distribution:** Bar chart across different age groups.  
- 📋 **Job Satisfaction Analysis:** Heatmap linking roles with satisfaction scores.  
- 📚 **Education Field Attrition:** Horizontal bar chart by field of study.  
- 👫 **Gender-Based Attrition Trends:** Donut charts for different age groups.

## <span id="project-lifecycle">🔄 Project Lifecycle & Workflow</span>  

1. **Data Collection** → Employee dataset with demographics & job satisfaction.  
2. **Data Cleaning** → Removal of duplicates, handling nulls, formatting fixes.  
3. **Data Modeling** → Joins and calculated fields for attrition KPIs.  
4. **Dashboard Design** → Layout with KPIs, charts, and filters.  
5. **Deployment** → Published as a Tableau workbook (.twbx) and dashboard snapshot.

## <span id="business-takeaways">📈 Key Business Takeaways</span>  

- **Attrition is highest in Sales (56%).**  
- **Life Sciences graduates** form the largest attrition group.  
- **Younger employees (25–34 years)** show higher attrition, especially males.  
- **Job roles with low satisfaction scores** correlate with higher attrition risk.
